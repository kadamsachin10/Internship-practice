## TOPIC 1: SDLC STUDY

### 1. Waterfall Model
- Software is developed step by step.
- One phase is completed before starting the next phase.
- Changes are difficult to make later.

### 2. Agile Model

**Definition:** Agile is a way of developing software in small parts and taking customer feedback regularly.

**Key Points:**
- Requirements are divided based on priority.
- High-priority requirements are developed first.
- Each part is developed and tested.
- The completed part is delivered to the customer.
- Customer feedback is used to improve the software.
- This process continues until the product is developed.

### 3. Scrum Basics

**What is Scrum?**
- Scrum is a framework used to manage Agile projects.
- Work is completed in short periods called Sprints.

**Scrum Events**
- Sprint
- Sprint Planning
- Daily Scrum
- Sprint Review
- Sprint Retrospective

**Product Backlog**
- A list of all requirements and features of the product.

**Sprint Backlog**
- A list of selected tasks to complete during a Sprint.

**Scrum Team Roles**
- Product Owner
- Scrum Master
- Developers

**Estimation**
- Estimation helps the team understand how much effort a task may need.

**Story Points**
- Story Points help estimate the effort and complexity of a task.

**Definition of Done (DoD)**
- Conditions that must be met before work is considered complete.

**Role of a Tester in Agile/Scrum**
- Understand requirements.
- Write and execute test cases.
- Find and report bugs.
- Retest fixed bugs.
- Work with developers to improve software quality.
